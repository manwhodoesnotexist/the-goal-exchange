World Cup Goal Exchange

Rules

Goal scored: +2 points

Goal conceded: -1 point

Clean Sheet: no penalty for conceded goals

VAR Check: +3 per goal scored

Extra Time: +2 per team in drawn matches

How to Play

Pick 4 teams (1 per pot). Earn points based on match results.

Use one chip per round before matches begin.

Chips

Clean Sheet: removes conceded penalties
